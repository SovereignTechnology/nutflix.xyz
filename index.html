Peer-to-peer video,
paid in ecash.

Nutflix is a Nostr-native peer-to-peer video network where seeders are paid per delivered chunk in Cashu ecash.

It looks like YouTube, not like a protocol demo. Watch a video and your wallet pays the peers keeping it seeded — a little at a time, chunk by chunk, as the video actually arrives.

Nutflix and NFX are experimental software, in active development. Protocols, formats and APIs change without notice — expect nothing stable.

The idea

Buffer = money

▶

Buffer is a spend bar

Prefetch depth is a spend decision. Pause the player and the payments stop. You only pay for chunks that actually arrive.

⚡

Pay per chunk

Every chunk that reaches your player carries a tiny Cashu payment to the peer that served it. No subscriptions, no invoices for things you didn't watch.

⊘

50/50 by default

Creators and the seeder pool split earnings evenly — enforced by the seeder requiring two locked sets of proofs, one for each side.

◈

Nostr-native

Discovery rides Nostr: manifests, availability beacons, comments, search and playlists are events — not rows in someone's database.

Nutflix

The working demo

The first working build. TypeScript on the Pear runtime: an Electron desktop app that seeds over Hypercore/Hyperswarm, a React interface, and a payment channel that settles as you watch. Stage 1 complete with 2,000+ passing tests.

NFX

The production rebuild

A Rust core speaks iroh natively, video ships as content-addressed CMAF segments, and the Tauri 2 viewer verifies every byte by sha256 in WASM before the player sees it — so a browser keeps playing from peers even after the origin disappears.

NFX-06iroh transport
NFX-07Payments, open mode
NFX-08Payments, licensed mode & vouchers
NFX-09Split-mint extension
NFX-10Web transport
NFX-11Registry & conformance
NFX-12Hypercore transport (optional)

Spec text is public domain (CC0). Anyone can implement a watcher, seeder, relay, mint or indexer against these documents alone.
